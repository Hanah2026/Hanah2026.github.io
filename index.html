ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Train with purpose.
Grow with discipline.

Hanah Neht Taekwondo Academy is a Kukkiwon Dojang focused on quality Taekwondo instruction, personal development and a strong martial-arts foundation.

Our training environment encourages discipline, courtesy, perseverance, self-control and an indomitable spirit while helping students improve their physical fitness and confidence.

Master Reynaldo Tanjuakio Jr.International Master Instructor • Master Recommender • Poom & Dan Examiner
